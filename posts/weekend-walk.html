A Weekend Walk Changed My Perspective

I had been stuck on a problem for two days. Lines of code blurred together. Nothing felt right. On Saturday morning, I closed the laptop and went for a walk without a destination in mind.

An hour later, halfway up a gentle hill, the solution appeared — not as code, but as a clear idea. I didn't need more complexity. I needed to simplify what I had already built.

Space creates clarity

Our brains keep working even when we're not at the keyboard. Stepping outside gives those background processes room to finish. The best ideas rarely arrive when you're forcing them.

Small rituals matter

Now I try to walk every day, even if it's just around the block. It's become less about exercise and more about protecting a little space for thinking.

If you're feeling stuck today, try closing the tab. The answer might be waiting for you outside.
